What We Build

Five systems. One complete patient journey.

Every automation we build targets a specific bottleneck — saving staff time and improving the customer experience.

💬

Smart Reminder System

Automated WhatsApp and SMS reminders sent exactly 24 hours and 2 hours before every appointment. Patients confirm or reschedule with one tap.

  • Personalised per patient and treatment type
  • Responses tracked automatically — no manual logging
  • Zero manual calls for your receptionist
🔄

No-Show Rescue Engine

When a patient cancels or ghosts, an AI-crafted rebooking message goes out within 30 minutes — with 3 available slots to choose from.

  • Recovers 40–60% of cancellations automatically
  • Warm, human-sounding messages via AI
  • Tracks recovery rate in your weekly dashboard
🔔

Patient Recall Automation

90 days after a patient's last visit, they receive a personalised message inviting them back — before they book with a competitor.

  • Works 24/7 without any manual input
  • Targets lapsed patients you've forgotten about
  • Links directly to your booking system
⭐

Google Review Automation

Post-treatment review requests sent at the perfect moment — when patient satisfaction is highest and the experience is fresh.

  • Helps generate more reviews through better timing
  • Timed for day-7 follow-up message
  • Direct link to your Google Business profile
📊

Weekly Performance Dashboard

Every Monday morning, you receive an automated report showing exactly what the system did — and what revenue it recovered — the previous week.

  • No-show rate, confirmation rate, reviews, recalls
  • Sent automatically via email every Monday
  • Simple to read — no technical knowledge needed
🤖

WhatsApp AI Chatbot

A 24/7 AI assistant that handles new patient enquiries from your clinic's WhatsApp — qualifying leads, answering FAQs, and booking appointments automatically.

  • Trained on your clinic's services and pricing
  • Hands off to staff when human touch is needed
  • Captures leads even at 2am
New

AI Voice Agent

A conversational AI that answers your clinic's phone calls 24/7 — booking appointments, handling FAQs, and routing urgent cases to staff — in your clinic's voice and language.

  • Handles inbound calls after-hours & on weekends
  • Books directly into your calendar — no hold time
  • Speaks naturally in English, Hindi, Arabic & more
  • Escalates emergencies to on-call staff instantly
  • Call summaries logged automatically to your dashboard

FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Do we need any special software or subscriptions?
+
No new software to install or learn on your end. Everything runs on our infrastructure. You don't pay extra platform subscriptions — everything is included in your setup fee. We connect to the tools you already use wherever possible.
Will this work with our existing booking system?
+
Yes, in almost all cases. We connect to most common booking and practice management systems, or we can set up a simple data entry process that works alongside your current workflow. We'll confirm compatibility during the free audit call before you commit to anything.
How long does setup really take?
+
7 working days from the time you confirm and pay the deposit. That's the typical timeline for the Smart Clinic OS. Enterprise setups with multiple locations or custom integrations may take 10–14 days. We've never missed a go-live deadline.
What languages does the AI Voice Agent support?
+
The AI Voice Agent currently supports English, Hindi, Arabic, and can be configured for other languages on request. It speaks in a natural, conversational tone — trained specifically on healthcare clinic call scenarios. It can handle mixed-language conversations (e.g. Hinglish) and will always escalate to a human staff member when the situation requires it.
What kind of results can I expect?
+
Every clinic is different. During your free audit we'll identify the specific areas where automation can create the biggest impact for your practice — whether that's reducing no-shows, recovering lapsed patients, saving staff time, or generating more reviews. We'll give you a clear, honest picture of what's realistic before you spend anything.
What currency will I be invoiced in?
+
We invoice in your local currency — INR, USD, GBP, AUD, AED, or any major currency. Prices displayed on this site are USD equivalents. The final invoice will be in your local currency at the current exchange rate, with no additional conversion fees added by us.
What happens to our patient data?
+
All patient data is stored securely and remains under your control at all times. We don't share patient information with third parties. We can work within HIPAA-aware, GDPR-compliant, and local data protection frameworks depending on your country. We're happy to sign an NDA or data processing agreement.
Do we need technical knowledge to use this?
+
None at all. Your receptionist uses a simple interface — add a new appointment, and the automations handle everything else. You get a weekly email report every Monday. That's it. No complex dashboards, no workflows to manage. The system runs silently in the background.
