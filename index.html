FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Do we need any special software or subscriptions?
+
No new software to install on your end. We use WhatsApp Business API, Airtable (free tier is enough for most clinics), and n8n running on our server. You don't pay extra platform subscriptions — everything is included in your setup fee.
Will this work with our existing booking system?
+
Yes, in almost all cases. We connect to most common booking and practice management systems, or we can use a simple Airtable form as the data entry point if needed. We'll confirm compatibility during the free audit call before you commit to anything.
How long does setup really take?
+
7 working days from the time you confirm and pay the deposit. That's the typical timeline for the Smart Clinic OS. Enterprise setups with multiple locations or custom integrations may take 10–14 days. We've never missed a go-live deadline.
What languages does the AI Voice Agent support?
+
The AI Voice Agent currently supports English, Hindi, Arabic, and can be configured for other languages on request. It speaks in a natural, conversational tone — trained specifically on healthcare clinic call scenarios. It can handle mixed-language conversations (e.g. Hinglish) and will always escalate to a human staff member when the situation requires it.
What's the 3× ROI guarantee?
+
If your Smart Clinic OS doesn't recover at least 3× the setup cost in revenue within 60 days of going live, we refund the difference. We measure this using the no-show data and recovery metrics tracked automatically in your Airtable dashboard. We stand behind the numbers because they're tracked live.
What currency will I be invoiced in?
+
We invoice in your local currency — INR, USD, GBP, AUD, AED, or any major currency. Prices displayed on this site are USD equivalents. The final invoice will be in your local currency at the current exchange rate, with no additional conversion fees added by us.
What happens to our patient data?
+
All patient data remains in your Airtable base — which you own and control. We don't store patient information on any third-party servers. We can work within HIPAA-aware, GDPR-compliant, and local data protection frameworks depending on your country. We're happy to sign an NDA or data processing agreement.
Do we need technical knowledge to use this?
+
None at all. Your receptionist sees a simple Airtable view — add a new appointment row, the automations handle everything else. You get a weekly email report. That's it. No dashboards to check, no workflows to manage. It runs silently in the background.
